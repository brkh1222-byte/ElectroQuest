A local news app needs short headlines. Each message is a short notice. Write a **system prompt** that replies with **one headline** for it.

The headline must mention the main subject. For example, a notice about the library must say "library".

You can also change **Max output tokens** in the settings bar. Be careful: a cut-off reply may lose the main word.

**Accepted:** at least 85% of hidden headlines mention the main subject, **and** the average reply is at most **14 output tokens**.
