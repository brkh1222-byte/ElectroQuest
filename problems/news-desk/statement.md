You help a news website sort its stories. Each message is a **real news story**: the headline on the first line and a short description on the second line.

Write a **system prompt** that replies with the story's **topic**, one of these 4:

- `world`: news about other countries, international events, storms and disasters abroad.
- `sports`: games, players, teams and competitions.
- `business`: the economy, jobs, companies, workers and money.
- `tech`: apps, phones, the internet and technology companies' products.

The whole reply must be just the topic, for example `sports`. Capital letters don't matter.

Some headlines are jokes or puns, so read the description too.

**Accepted:** at least 80% of hidden stories correct.
