## Hint 1
Tell the model what it must *output*, not only what the task is. Say what the reply should look like.

## Hint 2
Few-shot examples help a lot for borderline tickets, like "I was charged twice because the app crashed".

## Hint 3
Decide on a tiebreak rule: if a ticket mentions money, which label wins?

## Editorial
State the three labels with one-line definitions, give 3–6 short examples including a borderline one, and finish with: "Reply with only the label, in lowercase, with no other words."
