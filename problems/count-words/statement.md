A shop wants to know how long its customer reviews are. Write a **function** `count_words(text)` that **returns** the number of words in `text`.

- Words are separated by spaces. There may be extra spaces at the start, at the end, or between words.
- An empty text, or a text with only spaces, has `0` words.

```python
count_words("I love this phone")   # returns 4
count_words("  too   many   spaces  ")   # returns 3
```

Your function must **return** the number. Printing it is not enough.

**Accepted:** all tests pass.
