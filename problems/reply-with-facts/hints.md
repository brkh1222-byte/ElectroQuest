## Hint 1
Press **Run** and look at the average output tokens. Are the drafts long? Do they use the facts in the notes, or invent their own?

## Hint 2
Explain the message: "The message has a customer email and my notes. Write the reply using the facts in my notes." Then add: "Copy codes, prices, days and times exactly."

## Hint 3
Ask for a short shape: "Reply in 2 or 3 short sentences, under 50 words. No subject line and no placeholders like [Your Name]. Don't add facts or promises that are not in the notes."

## Editorial
Explain the two parts of the message, demand exact facts, and set a short length:

> The message contains a customer's email and my notes. Write a short, friendly reply to the customer that uses the facts in my notes. Include every fact from the notes, and copy codes, prices, days and times exactly. Do not add facts or promises that are not in the notes. Use 2 or 3 short sentences, under 50 words, and sign it "The Shop Team". No subject line and no placeholders.
