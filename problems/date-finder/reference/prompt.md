Find the date mentioned in the message.
Reply with it in the format YYYY-MM-DD, for example 2025-03-05.
Dates written with slashes or dots are day/month/year: 03/04/2025 means 2025-04-03.
If the message does not contain a complete date with a day, month and year (for example "next Tuesday", "tomorrow", or a month without a day), reply with exactly: none
Numbers that are not dates, like invoice or version numbers, do not count.
Reply with only the date or none, and nothing else.
