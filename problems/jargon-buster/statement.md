A school website has a "jargon buster" box. Each message asks about a technical term. Write a **system prompt** whose reply:

- explains the term for a **12-year-old**,
- is **one sentence** with an everyday comparison,
- **starts with "It's like"**.

**How replies are checked:** each reply must contain `It's like`, and the average reply must be at most **40 output tokens**.

**Accepted:** at least 85% of hidden replies contain "It's like", and the average is within the budget.
