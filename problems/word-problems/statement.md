A homework app needs a model that solves short math word problems. Write a **system prompt** so that, for each problem:

- the model may show its working, as much as it likes, but
- the **last line** of the reply is **only the final answer**: a plain number like `18`, with no words, units, currency symbols or punctuation.

Only the last non-empty line of the reply is graded. Bold markers like `**18**` and a trailing period are ignored, but `$18`, `18 dollars` or `Answer: 18` count as wrong.

All answers are whole numbers.

**Accepted:** at least 85% of the hidden problems answered correctly.
