A flashcard app makes one practice question from each piece of study notes. In each message, the **key term** is marked in bold, like `**evaporation**`. Write a **system prompt** whose reply has exactly two lines:

```text
Q: What do we call it when heated water turns into vapor?
A: evaporation
```

- The answer to the question must be the **bold term**.
- The last line must be `A:` followed by the bold term **exactly as written**: no extra words and no full sentence.

Only the **last line** is graded. Capital letters, bold markers and a final period are ignored, but `A: It is evaporation` or a missing `A:` count as wrong.

**Accepted:** at least 85% of hidden notes correct.
