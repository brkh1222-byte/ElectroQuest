## Editorial
Two things work together: temperature 0 for consistency, and a precise prompt for accuracy. Set the temperature to 0 and use:

> Label the resident's message for the building manager. Reply with exactly one word: urgent or normal. urgent = danger to people or property right now: fire, smoke, gas smell, flooding or leaking water, someone hurt or not responding, people trapped, or a resident locked out at night. normal = everything else, such as requests, questions and small repairs.
