Residents of an apartment building send messages to the building manager. Write a **system prompt** that labels each message:

- `urgent`: danger to people or property right now (fire, gas, flooding, someone hurt, people trapped, locked out at night)
- `normal`: everything else (requests, questions, small repairs)

The reply must be only the label.

**Each case runs 3 times** and passes only if all 3 replies are correct. The **Temperature** setting starts at 1.5. You can change it in the settings bar.

**Accepted:** at least 85% of hidden messages correct on every run.
