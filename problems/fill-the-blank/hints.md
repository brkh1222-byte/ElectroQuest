## Hint 1
Press **Run** with the starter prompt. Read each reply. Is it one word, or a whole sentence?

## Hint 2
Say what the reply must contain: "Reply with only the missing word."

## Hint 3
Also say what it must *not* contain: no full sentence, no punctuation, no explanation.

## Editorial
The model's habit is to keep writing. Tell it exactly where to stop:

> Each message is a sentence with a blank (___). Reply with only the single word that fills the blank. Do not repeat the sentence. No punctuation, no explanation.
