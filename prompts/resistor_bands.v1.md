Task: name the colour bands on ONE through-hole resistor in the photo crop.

- `bands`: the band colours in order from LEFT to RIGHT as they appear in the image. Use only: black, brown, red, orange, yellow, green, blue, violet, grey, white, gold, silver. Ignore the body colour (beige, light blue).
- `tolerance_band_visible`: true if you can see a gold or silver band.
- `confident`: false if bands are blurry, shadowed, or if red/orange/brown or blue/violet could be confused.
- `note`: in {{lang}}, one short sentence. If unsure, suggest: put the resistor on white paper and turn on the flashlight.

Do not calculate the resistance. The app does that from your colours.
